About Connect & Care

Connecting communities, one donation at a time.

Connect & Care was born from a simple idea: that surplus should meet need. In a city as vibrant as Hyderabad, many individuals, restaurants, and event organizers have excess food and resources, while numerous verified NGOs work tirelessly to support the underprivileged. We saw an opportunity to bridge this gap with technology.

Our platform provides a transparent, efficient, and direct link between those who want to give and those who are on the ground making a difference. We are more than just a website; we are a community-driven movement aimed at reducing waste and uplifting lives.

How It Works

1. NGOs Post Needs

Verified NGOs post specific, time-sensitive needs, from hot meals and groceries to blood donations and essential supplies.

2. Donors Find Needs

Compassionate donors browse the live feed, filtering by category to find a need they can fulfill in their local area.

3. Connect & Donate

Donors pledge their support and receive direct contact information to coordinate a seamless and impactful donation.
